# Employee Onboarding

## Week 1
- Set up your laptop and install required tools.
- Meet with your manager to review your 30-60-90 day plan.
- Complete required compliance training in Workday.

## Access & Tools
- Submit an IT ticket to request access to Snowflake, Tableau, and GitHub.
- Your manager must approve all access requests.
- VPN is required for all remote work.
